Plan around the weather
Water levels, access, and crowding can vary by season and rainfall. Check local notices before travelling, follow safety instructions, and never enter restricted water areas.

Courtallam waterfalls

Courtallam (also written Kutralam) is Tenkasi's best-known waterfall destination, set against the Western Ghats. The Main Falls, Five Falls, and Old Courtallam Falls each offer a different setting, and the flow can be especially impressive during the southwest monsoon season.

Waterfall access and bathing areas may be restricted when conditions are unsafe or during heavy rain. Follow local instructions, use designated routes, and take care on wet, slippery surfaces.

  • Go early: popular falls can become busy, particularly on holidays.
  • Check locally: access can change with rainfall and safety conditions.
  • Keep it clean: use bins and avoid leaving offerings or litter near the water.

Five Falls and Old Courtallam

These well-known Courtallam-area cascades make easy additions to a day spent exploring the town. Five Falls is named for its separate streams, while Old Courtallam is another popular spot among visitors and local families.

Facilities and access arrangements differ from one site to another. Check current local guidance before setting out, keep an eye on children around water, and use only approved bathing areas.

Gundar Dam and the foothills

Gundar Dam, near Courtallam, is a scenic stop surrounded by green slopes and Western Ghats views. It offers a quieter change of pace from the busy waterfall areas and can be paired with a broader drive around the region.

Check whether visitor access is permitted on the day. Reservoirs and dam structures are not swimming areas; respect barriers and posted safety notices.

Papanasam and Agasthiyar Falls

Papanasam, in the neighbouring Tirunelveli district, is a popular nearby day trip known for its river scenery and Agasthiyar Falls. The surrounding Western Ghats are ecologically sensitive and include areas with specific access rules.

Travel only to places open to visitors, follow forest and water-safety directions, and check current access conditions before making the drive. Do not enter restricted forest areas.

Manimuthar and the wider region

Manimuthar Dam and its surrounding scenery make another worthwhile excursion from the Tenkasi area. Pair it with a visit to nearby towns only after checking travel times and local access information, as conditions and restrictions can vary.

Carry drinking water, plan transport in advance, and leave the natural surroundings as you found them.

Travel thoughtfully: waterfalls and forest edges can be hazardous, especially after rain. Respect closures, avoid feeding wildlife, and take all rubbish with you.
